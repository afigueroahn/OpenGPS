# Privacy policy

This policy explains what data {{app}} processes, what it uses it for, who it shares it with and what you can do with it.

## 2. What data we process

- **Your account:** your name, your email, your password and your photo, if you upload one. The password is stored encrypted in a way we cannot read.
- **Your sessions:** the IP address and the browser you sign in from, to protect your account; the date of your last access and whether you signed in with your email or with another service, which whoever runs the platform sees.
- **Your vehicles:** the name, plate and group you give them.
- **Your GPS devices:** their identifier (IMEI or ID) and their brand.
- **Location and operation:** the positions each device sends with their time, speed and heading, and the data the device reports, such as ignition, voltage, battery, satellites and signal.
- **Geofences, alerts and events:** the zones and routes you draw, the alerts you set up and what the platform records from them: each entry, exit or notice of a vehicle, with its time and location.
- **Drivers**, if your account uses that feature: the name, license, phone and identification key code you enter for each one; which vehicle they drove and when; and what the platform records about their driving: harsh acceleration and braking, aggressive turns, speeding and time at the wheel, with the resulting score.
- **Security**, if your account uses that feature: the orders to immobilize and release the ignition, with who gave them; each vehicle's incidents with their notes; and the files the people in your account attach as evidence, such as photos or a police report.
- **Fuel**, if your account uses that feature: the tank capacity and the sensor settings you enter. The level and consumption are calculated from the data the device already sends.
- **Activity:** a log of the operations done in your account (who added, changed, deleted or exported something, when and from which IP).
- **The people you invite:** their email, to send them the invitation.

We do not ask for or store payment details or identity documents.

## 3. What we use it for

- To provide you the service: show your vehicles on the map, keep their history and apply the permissions you define.
- To send you necessary emails: verification of your email, password recovery and invitations.
- To tell you about what you ask us to watch: alerts that have email turned on reach the people in the account who can see that vehicle. Each person can turn those emails off in their user menu.
- To send you the reports you schedule: they arrive by email, as an attachment, only to the people in your account you choose, and they carry what the person who scheduled them can see.
- To protect the service: detect abuse, investigate reports and comply with legal obligations.
- To improve the platform, with general statistics that do not identify you.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

## 4. Who it is shared with

We work with providers that process data on our behalf, only to provide the service:

- **Hosting:** the platform and its database are on the servers hired by whoever provides the service.
- **Network and site protection:** if the service uses a content delivery network (for example Cloudflare), the traffic of the website and the apps goes through it, which protects and speeds it up.
- **Email delivery:** the outgoing email provider used by the service sends the platform's emails. It receives your email address and the content of the message.
- **OpenStreetMap (Nominatim):** turns coordinates into an approximate address. It receives the coordinate of the vehicle you look up, without your name, your email or any data from your account.
- **Backup storage:** when it is turned on, backups are also kept with an external storage provider, which receives the complete backups.
- **OpenFreeMap:** serves the background map. Your browser requests it directly, so that service sees your IP address and the area of the map you look at, but not your vehicles.

In addition:

- The people you invite to your account see your vehicles according to the role and groups you assign them.
- If you create a link to share a vehicle's location, anyone who has that link sees where that vehicle is, its name, its speed and its last report, and nothing else. The link stops working when it expires or when you revoke it.
- We will hand data over to an authority only when the law requires us to and it is requested through legal channels.

## 5. Where it is stored

The data is stored on the servers where whoever provides the service installs the platform. If they are in a country other than yours, by using it your data goes there.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Your account and your vehicles:** while you have the account.
- **Position history:** while the vehicle and its device exist. Today it has no maximum period; if we set one, we will tell you beforehand.
- **Backups:** automatic ones are kept for 7 days and then deleted on their own. If that period changes, we will tell you. A backup made by hand by whoever administers the service is kept until it is deleted.
- **When you remove a device or delete a vehicle:** its position history and its events are deleted. Deleting a vehicle also deletes its incidents with their evidence, its immobilization orders, its tank settings and what was recorded of the driving in that vehicle.
- **A driver's record:** until you delete it. When you delete it, their personal data disappears; what they drove stays in the reports as driven without an assigned driver.
- **The recovery mode link:** stops working after 72 hours, or earlier if you turn that mode off.
- **When you delete your account:** your vehicles, devices, history, geofences, alerts, events, drivers, incidents with their evidence, scheduled reports, dashboards, groups, invited users and the activity log are deleted. Automatic backups that contain them disappear when their period ends.

We may keep some data for longer only if a law requires us to or if it is needed to handle a report in progress.

## 7. How we protect it

- All communication with the platform is encrypted.
- Each account's data is kept apart: one account cannot see another's.
- Access to the servers is restricted, and every change in an account is recorded in a log that cannot be altered.
- We make daily backups.

No measure is infallible. If an incident affects your data, we will tell you.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on the law of your country, you can ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, object to a use or take it with you. On the platform you can do much of this yourself:

- Change the details of your vehicles and your account.
- Remove a device or delete a vehicle, with its history.
- Download a vehicle's history to a file.
- Delete your whole account, from your profile menu.

For any other request, write to us at {{contact}}. We will answer within a reasonable time.

## 9. If you track vehicles driven by other people

A vehicle's location can reveal where its driver is. If that person is not you, it is up to you to tell them the vehicle is tracked and to comply with the laws of your country. Using the platform to track someone without their knowledge is not allowed.

If you also register your drivers, you keep personal data about them and the platform measures and scores how they drive. You are the one who decides to process that data and who answers for it: tell them what is recorded and why, and handle their requests. We only process it on your behalf, to provide you the service.

## 10. Minors

The platform is not intended for people under 18, and they must not create an account.

## 11. Cookies

We only use the cookie needed to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies. If the service uses a content delivery network, it may add technical cookies to protect the site.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you on the platform before the change takes effect.
