# App Store — English (U.S.)

## Name

OpenGPS

## Subtitle

GPS tracking for your vehicles

## Promotional text

Open and free. Connect to your OpenGPS platform and follow your vehicles from your phone: the live map, their history and their alerts.

## Description

OpenGPS shows you where your vehicles are, live, from your phone.

It works with an OpenGPS platform: the one at app.opengps.dev, where you create your account for free, or the one your company installs. Add your GPS devices (Teltonika, Concox, SinoTrack, Coban, Queclink, Suntech and more) and watch them move on the map.

ON THE MAP
• Your vehicles live, with their status: moving, idling, stopped or offline.
• The address where each one is.
• Share a vehicle's location with a link that expires.

HISTORY
• Go over the trips and stops of any period, and play them back on the map.
• Export the route and share it as a file.

ALERTS AND INCIDENTS
• See your vehicles' alerts: speeding, geofences, ignition and more.
• Report incidents and follow them until they are resolved, with their evidence.

SECURITY
• Lock the engine remotely on devices that support it, with a double confirmation.
• Sign in with a passkey or your password, and change it from the app.

YOUR ACCOUNT
• Add a GPS device from your phone.
• Switch accounts if you manage several, and choose the language and a light or dark theme.
• Delete your account and all its data whenever you want, from the app.

Some features depend on the modules enabled in your account.

OpenGPS is free software (Apache-2.0). The app shows no ads and uses no trackers, and it does not ask for your phone's location: it shows your GPS devices' location.
